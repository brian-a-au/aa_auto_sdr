# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its community Code of Conduct.

Everyone who takes part in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests,
reviews, project discussions, and other spaces where they represent the
project is expected to follow that covenant. We want a community that is
welcoming and free of harassment.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately
through the repository's
[private reporting channel](https://github.com/brian-a-au/aa_auto_sdr/security/advisories/new).
Do not put a conduct report in a public issue or pull request.

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards and
will review each report fairly and promptly. The maintainer will respect the
privacy of everyone involved as far as possible while looking into and
resolving a report. The maintainer may remove or edit contributions and may
issue a correction, a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban, in line
with the Contributor Covenant's community impact guidelines.

Anyone who handles a report must disclose a conflict of interest. They must
step aside when they cannot act fairly. A reporter or an affected participant
may ask for the decision to be reconsidered through the same private channel.
